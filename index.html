About the Lab

Physics Lab was created to make learning physics easier, more understandable, and more interesting for students. Physics can sometimes feel difficult because it involves many formulas, concepts, and ideas that are hard to visualize. I wanted to create a place where students could explore these ideas in a simpler way.
