Energy & Work

Energy is one of the most important concepts in physics. It allows objects and systems to cause changes, move, heat up, or interact with their surroundings.


What Is Energy?

Energy is the ability of a system to cause change or perform work. Energy can exist in many different forms, including kinetic, potential, thermal, chemical, electrical, and nuclear energy.


Kinetic Energy

Kinetic energy is the energy an object has because of its motion. An object's kinetic energy increases when its mass or speed increases.


The kinetic energy of an object depends strongly on its speed. This is why a fast-moving object can have much more kinetic energy than a slow-moving object with the same mass.


Potential Energy

Potential energy is stored energy. Gravitational potential energy depends on an object's mass, its height, and the gravitational field. A raised object has the potential to release energy as it falls.


Work

In physics, work occurs when a force causes an object to move through a distance in the direction of the force. Work is measured in joules.


Power

Power describes how quickly work is done or energy is transferred. Two machines may perform the same amount of work, but the machine that does it in less time has greater power.


Conservation of Energy

The law of conservation of energy states that energy cannot be created or destroyed. Instead, it can be transferred from one object or system to another or transformed from one form into another.


Energy Transformations

Energy transformations happen constantly. For example, a roller coaster can transform gravitational potential energy into kinetic energy as it moves downhill. In a light bulb, electrical energy is transformed mainly into light and thermal energy.


Efficiency

Real systems often transfer some energy into unwanted forms, such as thermal energy caused by friction. Efficiency compares useful energy output with the total energy supplied to a system.


Key Ideas

Energy can be stored, transferred, and transformed. Work describes energy transferred by a force through a distance, while power describes how quickly that transfer occurs.
